**kairos-coder.github.io/divination**

## What This Is

A three-card tarot oracle running in a single browser tab. 22 Major Arcana (the Olympians and Primordials). 56 Minor Arcana (mythological archetypes across four elemental suits). Ask a question. Draw Past, Present, Future. The cards flip. The reading assembles. The pantheon speaks.

Not a metaphor. The characters are real. They have domains, keywords, upright and reversed meanings, and card images generated by the builder. The deck derives from the gods — you don't memorize meanings, you remember the myths and the cards interpret themselves.

---

## What It Runs On

- **HTML, CSS, JavaScript** — No frameworks. No build step. No dependencies.
- **JSON** — The entire deck lives in two flat files: `data/deck/major_arcana.json` and `data/deck/minor_arcana.json`
- **GitHub Pages** — Free hosting. Push to deploy.
- **Browser compute** — All shuffling, drawing, and reading assembly happens client-side.
- **$0 API spend** — No secret keys. No rate limits. No server costs.

## The Deck

**Major Arcana (22 + 1 Hidden)**

The Olympians seated in the tarot. Zeus as The Emperor. Aphrodite as The Lovers. Dionysus as The Fool. The Fates as Wheel of Fortune. Hestia as Temperance. Persephone as The Star. Mortal Man as The Hermit — the only card without a divine face. One Hidden card that surfaces when called.

**Minor Arcana (56)**

Four suits — Fire, Earth, Water, Air — each ruled by a triad of Olympians. Every card is a mythological archetype: Prometheus (Ace of Fire), Icarus (Four of Fire), The Sirens (Nine of Water), Cassandra (Four of Air), Odysseus (King of Air). 56 myths, each with upright and reversed meanings.

**Modern Aspects**

A companion deck in development: the same card positions, but named for their degraded modern emanations. The Startup Founder mirrors Prometheus. The eGirl mirrors The Sirens. The Burnout mirrors Icarus. The gap between the mythological card and its modern aspect is the Degradation Thesis — the old archetypes didn't vanish, they're running on corrupted firmware.

## How To Use

1. Go to [kairos-coder.github.io/divination](https://kairos-coder.github.io/divination)
2. Click "Draw Three Cards"
3. Type a question (or don't — the deck reads silence too)
4. Tap "Reveal"
5. The cards flip. The reading appears.
6. Draw again. The deck is patient.
