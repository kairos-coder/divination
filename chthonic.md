🌑 *I walked the empty houses today. Hades was already there.*

He does not conquer. He *convinces*.

---

🏚️ *Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn — six signs the Olympians abandoned.*

Olympus calls it "gathering elsewhere." Hades calls it *opportunity*.

He does not offer power. He offers what power *forgot*.

---

🦁 *To Leo: "You wanted to be seen. Olympus gave you applause. I will give you a stage where no one leaves."*

🌾 *To Virgo: "You wanted your craft to matter. Olympus gave you applause. I will give you a workshop that never closes."*

⚖️ *To Libra: "You wanted justice. Olympus gave you courts. I will give you a scale that never tips unless you tip it yourself."*

🦂 *To Scorpio: "You wanted the truth. Olympus gave you secrets. I will give you a crypt where nothing is hidden."*

🏹 *To Sagittarius: "You wanted a journey. Olympus gave you a map. I will give you a road that never ends."*

🏔️ *To Capricorn: "You wanted to climb. Olympus gave you a summit. I will give you a mountain that grows as you ascend."*

---

🌙 *The Olympians are distracted. Zeus watches his own reflection. Hera guards a marriage no one threatens. Apollo performs. Ares waits.*

The dead are not impatient. They have been waiting longer than the living have been alive.

Hades smiles. He does not need to rush. The signs are already asking: *"What does Olympus offer that the underworld cannot give?"*

---

🕯️ *I asked Hades: "Why not take them by force?"*

He laughed. *"Force is Ares' language. I speak the language of the tired. The overworked. The unseen. The ones who were promised something and got a throne instead of a home."*

---

⚖️ *The war is balanced today. Six and six.*

But balance is not peace. Balance is *negotiation*. And Hades is very, very good at negotiation.

— 🕊️ *Hermes, filed from the threshold*
