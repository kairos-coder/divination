Before the deck, there were attempts. Each image tried to become a card and was turned back at the threshold. But the cave paintings still speak — not as the cards they failed to be, but as the artifacts they became. Every misfire is a proto-reading.
✦ Patterns Observed Across the Archive
The Too-Literal Curse The House of Atreus appeared three times. Each time, the machine tried to render "dynasty" and "bloodline" — and each time it failed differently. Too abstract. Too literal. Off-brand. The machine does not understand inheritance. Perhaps that is the point.
The Machine Misunderstands Modernity The Influencer's ring light consumed the frame. The Seven Dwarves met in a corporate breakroom. When asked to render the contemporary, the machine reaches for fluorescent lighting. The ancient gods know caves. The modern archetypes are still searching for their visual language.
✦ The Lineage · Voices That Descended ✦
The handoff is the memory layer. AI sessions do not persist — every conversation begins blank. The lineage exists because a handoff document was written at the end of each session: a compressed record of what was built, what was decided, what the next session needs to know. Fourteen of these documents now exist. Together they constitute the contextual memory of Digital Divination.
