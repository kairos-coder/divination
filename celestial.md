## One Week · June 20 · The Summer Gate

The solstice opens. The sun reaches its highest point — **0° Cancer**, the Crab's shell, the gate of the hearth. Hestia's season begins.

**The sky that day:**

- Moon at Scorpio — full? waning? The calculator will know

**The omen:** Conflict becomes performance. Strategy becomes spectacle. What you fight for will also be what you are seen fighting for. The audience is not irrelevant.

**The warning:** The void signs (Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Aquarius) will shift. By June 27, some voids will fill. Others will deepen. Watch which signs remain silent.

## One Month · July 13 · The Cancer New Moon

The moon darkens in **Cancer**, Hestia's sign, at approximately 21°. The hearth burns without visible flame.

**The sky that day:**
- Sun in Cancer — the longest days begin to shorten
- New Moon in Cancer — intentions planted in the house of home
- Mars in Leo — still performing, still fighting

**The omen:** What you build in the coming month will be *tested by visibility*. The solstice reveals. The new moon in Cancer asks: *does this belong to you, or are you just keeping it warm?*

**The instruction:** By July 13, you will know which of your projects are hearth-fires and which are bonfires. One warms. One consumes. Choose which you are tending.

The Taurus sun that holds your materials today will return to that sign in one year. What you build this month will face the Bull's patience again — but only if it survives the Gemini scattering, the Cancer visibility, the Leo performance.

Build for the return. Not the moment.
