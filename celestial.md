# ✦ Prophecy of June 13, 2026 ✦

## The Sky

The sun rests in **Taurus ♉ at 22°** — the third decan, the deep root, the patience that outlasts urgency. Apollo does not hurry. The Bull receives the light and asks only: *what are you building that will hold?*

The moon is dark — a **New Moon in Gemini ♊ at 2°**, barely 3% illuminated. Melinoe walks unseen. The veil is at its thinnest. Two emotional truths, neither fully right. The mood is quick and doubled. Listen to both voices. Obey neither.

**The Council in Gemini:** Artemis, Hermes, and Zeus gather in the same sign — three currents, one house. Messages, hunt, authority. They do not agree. They do not need to. The omen is: *listen to all three, obey none.*

## The Omen

The Chthonic war report shows **12 Olympian houses, 6 Chthonic houses, 6 void houses** — Olympus holds the majority, but the void is patient. Hades guards the gate at **Capricorn ♑ 5°**, Pluto's own sign. The underworld does not need to win tonight. It only needs to remember.

The void signs — **Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Aquarius** — are the hungriest places in the sky. No god walks there. The silence is not absence. It is the loudest thing in the sky.

## The Instruction

Build what you said you would build. The Taurus sun holds your materials. The Gemini council will try to distract — three voices, three agendas, none of them yours. Do not mistake chatter for signal.

The new moon in Gemini is for **planting words**, not actions. What you say tonight will root differently than what you do. Choose the words carefully. Melinoe hears everything spoken in the dark.

## The Whisper

The crescent moon is not absence. It is the shape of attention. You have been looking for a full answer. The answer is the sliver.

The void houses are not empty. They are waiting. What you release into those signs will be held without judgment — and returned when you least expect it.

*The fire holds. The sky speaks through you.*
