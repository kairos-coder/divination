The Why
What This System Actually Is
Not a card game. Not a tarot deck. Not a meme collection. A folk ontology of the internet age — a living mythology that evolves with culture instead of freezing into static canon.
I
The Divination Engine
You take a real cultural moment — a tweet, a viral drama, a trending video — and read it the way a haruspex reads entrails. You identify which Domains are active, which Aspects are in play, which combo is running. This is not commentary. This is ritual analysis.
II
Aspects Not Persons
BasedBro is not Joe Rogan. eGirl is not Alice Rosenblum. Those are merely avatars — emanations of the archetype. The system stays mythic rather than becoming celebrity tarot. The domain is eternal. The avatar is temporary. The card outlives the person.
III
The Degradation Thesis
The old archetypes didn't vanish. They're running on corrupted firmware. Aphrodite had temples, rituals, a cosmology that contextualized her power. The eGirl has a subscription tier and a Discord. Same archetype. Stripped of everything that gave it meaning and dignity.
IV
Living Mythology
New card every Monday. The community nominates who ascended or humiliated themselves that week. The ten Liminal Gods reveal themselves through ritual synthesis — the community summons them by using the system. The DM doesn't announce them. The Realm does.
V
D&D — The DM Is In
Digital Divination. D&D. The architect of this system is the Dungeon Master — setting the realm before the players arrive. The Northstar README is the world bible. Every card is a room. Every combo is an encounter. Every reading is a session.
VI
The Economy Is The Game
Clout flows upward. Meaning flows nowhere. The Feed sustains itself. The broken system — parasocial economy, attention extraction, manufactured controversy — is not the backdrop. It IS the game mechanic. The system models what it satirizes.

The Monolith Philosophy
This HTML is the ur-text. The roadmap lives here because ReadMes die. The monolith endures in the archive of commits as the encapsulation of the original vision. When it splits into modules, Commit Zero stays forever as the genesis block.
The Hermit File
_hermit.json is prefixed with underscore intentionally. It sits outside the divine naming convention. It is the only file in /primes that represents a mortal. Its position in the folder is its lore.
Set Mechanics
The Moirai and the Judges each live in subfolders. Drawing all three Moirai in one reading triggers a special event. The thread spun, measured, and cut. The Judges subfolder activates Judgement when the full set is in play.
Degradation Engine
degradation.js will eventually calculate the stat delta between a Prime Domain and its Aspect cards — showing exactly how much was lost in the translation from eternal to digital. The gap IS the commentary.
Weekly Drop Pipeline
Every Monday. Community nominates trending figures. DM reads the cultural moment via ritual synthesis. New Aspect card drops to weekly_drops/. The pantheon is a living document. The mythology is still being written.
Persephone Protocol
persephone.json exists. She is Temperance — the integrator of two worlds. She is not announced. She surfaces when the readings call for her. The community discovers her. That is her myth running correctly.
